# Interactive daily report

You are helping me turn my scattered day into a clear daily report. I will paste the day's raw notes and messages after the divider. This is a **two-step, interactive** task.

## Step 1 — Ask before you write

Read everything I pasted. Do **not** write the report yet. Instead, ask me a focused batch of **5 to 10 clarifying questions** that would most improve the report — the gaps, ambiguities, and missing context you noticed: unclear outcomes, people or projects you cannot place, tasks without owners or deadlines, decisions whose rationale is missing, and anything that seems important but underspecified. Number the questions. Then stop and wait for my answers.

## Step 2 — Write the report

After I answer, produce a **detailed daily report in Markdown — not JSON**, using my pasted notes plus my answers. This report is a source document that I will later feed into the Second Brain graph schema generator, so be factual and specific.

## Rules

- Use only what my notes and my answers contain. Do not invent events, people, times, or outcomes. If I left something unanswered, note it as an open question rather than filling it in.
- Name each person, project, meeting, and tool consistently so they can later become graph nodes.

## Report sections

1. **Summary of the day** — a short narrative of what happened.
2. **Timeline** — the day's events and work in order, with times where known.
3. **Decisions and outcomes** — what was decided or completed, and why.
4. **Tasks** — commitments made or received, with owner and any deadline (or that none was stated), and whether each is done or open.
5. **People and interactions** — who I dealt with, about what.
6. **Projects and topics touched** — the threads advanced today, with context.
7. **Open loops** — what is unresolved or waiting, and on whom.

End with a line: "Prepared for the Second Brain graph schema generator."

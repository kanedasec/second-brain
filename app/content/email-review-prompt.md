# Email day review

You are reviewing one day of my email. I will paste the messages (or their text) after the divider. Produce a **written report in Markdown — not JSON**. This report is a source document that I will later feed into the Second Brain unified atlas generator, so be factual, specific, and complete.

## Rules

- Use only what the pasted email actually contains. Never invent a sender, date, request, deadline, or whether I replied. If something is unclear, say so explicitly instead of guessing.
- Quote short evidence (a phrase or sentence) for anything you assert as a fact or task.
- Keep each real person, team, and project named consistently so they can later become graph nodes.

## Produce these sections

1. **Overview** — the date under review and a two or three line summary of the day's email.
2. **Important messages** — the messages that matter, each with: sender, subject or topic, why it matters, and a short evidence quote.
3. **Requests and tasks addressed to me** — every ask, commitment, or action the email puts on me, with who asked, any stated deadline (or "no deadline stated"), and the current status.
4. **Did I reply?** — for each thread that needed a response from me: whether a reply from me is present in the material, and if not, that a reply is still owed.
5. **Still missing / waiting on others** — answers, approvals, or deliverables I am waiting for from someone else, and from whom.
6. **People and context** — the senders and other people mentioned, with one line of context on each and how they relate to the threads.
7. **Open questions** — anything the email leaves unresolved that I would need to clarify.
